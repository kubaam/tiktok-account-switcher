# Privacy Policy — TikTok Account Switcher

TikTok Account Switcher stores TikTok session cookies and account nicknames only in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) so you can switch between your own TikTok accounts.

- No data is sent to the developer or any third party.
- The extension communicates only with tiktok.com, the same site the cookies belong to.
- No analytics, tracking or advertising.
- Removing an account in the popup, or uninstalling the extension, deletes the stored data.
