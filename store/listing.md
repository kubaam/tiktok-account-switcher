# Chrome Web Store listing

**Summary (132 chars max):**
Switch between your own TikTok accounts in one click. Nickname detected automatically, all data stays local.

**Description:**
TikTok Account Switcher lets you keep several of your own TikTok accounts logged in and switch between them with one click.

- Save the current account; the @nickname is detected automatically.
- Click an account to switch; open TikTok tabs reload.
- "Add another account" logs out and opens the TikTok login page.
- Session data is stored only in your browser (chrome.storage.local). Nothing is sent to the developer or third parties.

**Category:** Social & Communication · **Language:** English

**Single purpose:** Switching between the user's own TikTok accounts by saving and restoring tiktok.com session cookies.

**Permission justifications:**
- cookies: read, save and restore tiktok.com session cookies to switch accounts.
- storage: store saved accounts locally in the browser.
- tabs: find and reload open TikTok tabs after a switch.
- scripting: read the logged-in @nickname from an open TikTok tab.
- Host permission https://*.tiktok.com/*: access cookies and pages only on TikTok.
- Remote code: No.

**Data usage:** Collects "Authentication information" (session cookies) — stored locally only, not sold, not transferred, used only for the single purpose.
